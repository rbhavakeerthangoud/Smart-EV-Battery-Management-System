# Hardware Components

## STM32 Microcontroller

The STM32 microcontroller is the main control unit of the system. It reads sensor data, processes the measurements, displays system information, and controls the safety relays.

## 3S Battery Pack

The 3S battery pack is used as the monitored battery system in the project.

## Voltage Sensor

The voltage sensor measures the battery voltage and sends the measured value to the STM32.

## ACS712 Current Sensor

The ACS712 sensor measures the current flowing through the battery during charging and discharging.

## DHT11 Temperature Sensor

The DHT11 sensor monitors temperature and helps detect abnormal thermal conditions.

## LCD Display

The LCD displays important information such as voltage, current, temperature, and battery status.

## Relay Module

The relay provides protective control by disconnecting the battery or circuit when abnormal conditions are detected.

## Charging Circuit

The charging circuit is used to charge the battery pack under monitored conditions.
